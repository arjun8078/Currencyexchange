Hi [Lead Name] / [PM Name],

I researched three online services that give us currency conversion rates (for example: convert Indian Rupees to US Dollars or UAE Dirhams) for our product.

The three services were:
1. Open Exchange Rates — https://openexchangerates.org/
2. ExchangeRate-API — https://www.exchangerate-api.com/
3. CurrencyAPI / free community feeds (okay for demos only — not for a company product)

I compared them with our reference numbers (22 Oct 2026: 10,000 INR ≈ 105.43 USD / 388.90 AED). Please see the attached PDF: Currency_Conversion_Service_Recommendation.pdf.

About the free versions
Both Open Exchange Rates and ExchangeRate-API have free plans that are useful for testing / POC:
- Open Exchange Rates free: about 1,000 requests per month, mainly today’s / latest rates
- ExchangeRate-API free: about 1,500 requests per month, mainly today’s / latest rates

So on free, ExchangeRate-API gives a slightly higher monthly call limit. Important: free plans are mostly for latest rates. Reliable conversion using a past date (invoice / booking date) generally needs a paid plan on both services. Free community feeds should not be used for a real company product.

My recommendation
- For production (paid): Open Exchange Rates as our main service
- If price is the main concern (paid): ExchangeRate-API as the budget alternative
- For testing now (free): either free plan is fine for a POC; ExchangeRate-API free allows a few more monthly calls

Why Open Exchange Rates over ExchangeRate-API for production
- Better support for converting using a past date (important for invoices / bookings), not only today’s rate
- Better fit for a company product (clear paid service, commonly used by application backends)
- Easier to keep a proper record: save which rate we used and on which date
- Covers the currencies we need (INR, USD, AED, SAR, and more)
