Thank you for pointing to the Frankfurter v2 API. That was the key detail.

Earlier confusion
Older Frankfurter v1 (ECB-only) does not include AED/SAR, so our first check looked incomplete. Frankfurter v2 does support AED and SAR with a date parameter. We verified the sample you shared:

https://api.frankfurter.dev/v2/rates?base=INR&quotes=AED&date=2026-10-20
→ rate 0.03872 → 10,000 INR = 387.20 AED

Our POC also got USD and SAR on the same date, and today’s AED was close to our reference pack (~388.90).

About free vs paid
- Frankfurter v2: free, no API key, date-based rates, includes USD/AED/SAR — strongest fit for cost right now
- Open Exchange Rates free: about 1,000 requests/month, mainly latest rates; paid needed for stronger commercial/historical SLA
- ExchangeRate-API free: about 1,500 requests/month, mainly latest rates; paid needed for reliable historical on their side

Recommendation
I now recommend Frankfurter v2 as our default conversion service for the product, because it is free, supports AED/SAR, supports conversion by date, and matched our POC numbers well.

If the company later needs a paid vendor contract / formal SLA, we can switch to:
- Open Exchange Rates (preferred paid option), or
- ExchangeRate-API (if price is the main constraint)

Our test app is already wired to Frankfurter v2. Please see the attached PDF: Currency_Conversion_Service_Recommendation.pdf.
