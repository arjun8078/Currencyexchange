I researched three online services that give us currency conversion rates (for example: convert Indian Rupees to US Dollars or UAE Dirhams) for our product.

The three services were:
1. Open Exchange Rates — https://openexchangerates.org/
2. ExchangeRate-API — https://www.exchangerate-api.com/
3. CurrencyAPI / free community feeds (okay for demos only — not for a company product)

I compared them with our reference numbers (22 Oct 2026: 10,000 INR ≈ 105.43 USD / 388.90 AED). Please see the attached PDF: Currency_Conversion_Service_Recommendation.pdf.

My recommendation
I recommend Open Exchange Rates (paid plan) as our main service for production.

Why Open Exchange Rates instead of ExchangeRate-API
- Better support for converting using a past date (important for invoices / bookings on a specific day), not only today’s rate.
- Better fit for a company product: clearer paid service, commonly used by application backends.
- Easier to keep a proper record: we can save “which rate we used and on which date” on each transaction.
- Covers the currencies we need (INR, USD, AED, SAR, and more) in one standard approach.

If price is the main concern
We can use ExchangeRate-API (paid) instead. In our tests accuracy was close, and it is usually a bit cheaper. Our app can switch to it with a simple setting. I would treat it as the budget option — not the free community feeds.
